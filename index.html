About Me

Curious about mathematics, driven by real impact.

I am currently in my third year studying Data Science and Applied Mathematics and Statistics (AMS) at the Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC). My journey started with a deep fascination for physics and math competitions, which earned me a provincial 1st runner-up and national 12th place finish in 2023.

Rather than keeping math on paper, I love building things that solve immediate problems. Recently, my team won the JICA Siem Reap Smart City Proof of Concept (PoC) by training edge computer vision models on municipal CCTV streams to assist traffic safety. Earlier, I created Your Hope, a bilingual mental health screener integrated with the Claude and Gemini APIs.

When I'm not studying or coding, I teach physics and foundational programming to kids at the School of Mathematics and Science. I am currently seeking internship and collaborative engineering opportunities where I can contribute analytical rigor and learn from experienced mentors.
